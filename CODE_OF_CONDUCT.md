# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the
community, and protect private or sensitive information. Harassment, threats,
sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately through the repository's maintainer contact or
GitHub reporting facilities. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately,
and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.
